A Letter from Rushabh

Dear Medhavi,

Happy 15th day to us, my favourite beginning.

It may be only fifteen days on the calendar, but these days deserve a little celebration of their own. I wanted to make you something different, something you could open whenever you wanted a reminder that you are appreciated.

I don’t know every chapter our story will have, and that is part of what makes it beautiful. I look forward to the conversations we have not had yet, the laughter we have not shared yet, the places we might discover, and the memories that are still waiting for us.

I hope I can keep giving you reasons to smile, keep learning what makes you happy, and keep showing up with care and honesty.

No pressure for perfection. No promises written just to sound beautiful. Just a genuine wish to make the days ahead meaningful, one little moment at a time.

Thank you for being you.

Happy 15 days, Medhavi.

With all my affection, Rushabh
